# PharmaBI Business Questions

The purpose of PharmaBI is to support business decisions through data.
Analyses should therefore originate from explicit business questions rather
than from available charts or statistical techniques.

## 1. Commercial Performance

- How much revenue, cost and profit does the business generate?
- What is the overall gross margin?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products sell the highest number of packages?
- Which products appear in the highest number of receipts?
- Which products combine high sales volume and high profitability?
- Which products have high margins but low sales volume?
- Which products have high sales volume but low profitability?

## 2. Product Categories

- Which GM categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which GM categories generate the highest profit?
- Which categories account for the largest sales volume?
- How does category performance change over time?
- Which ATC categories account for the greatest observed pharmaceutical demand?

## 3. Customer Purchasing Behaviour

- What is the average receipt value?
- What is the average number of products/packages per receipt?
- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- Which GM or ATC categories frequently occur in the same receipt?
- Are there products that act as recurring anchors in customer baskets?

## 4. Time and Operational Patterns

- Which days generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which time slots generate the highest sales activity?
- How does demand change across weeks and months?
- Are specific product categories concentrated in particular periods or time slots?

## 5. Inventory and Purchasing

To be developed after purchase data integration.

- Which products have the highest turnover?
- Which products may be overstocked?
- Which products may be at risk of stock-out?
- How closely do purchases follow actual sales demand?
- Can reorder decisions be improved using historical demand?

## 6. Seasonality and Long-Term Demand

To be expanded with additional years of data.

- Which products and categories show seasonal sales patterns?
- How stable are these patterns across years?
- Are changes in seasonal demand associated with weather, temperature or pollen conditions?
